    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Do you need my water or power?

    Yes. We use your water and power, and what we need depends on the package.

    Exterior or Full DetailOutdoor spigot+Outlet
    Interior onlyOutlet

    Good to know

    • The spigot is a must for any exterior work. No spigot? We can still do an Interior detail.
    • An indoor outlet works too if we can run an extension cord outside to the car.
    • The booking form asks about both, so we show up ready.
    How long does it take?

    Every detail is done by hand by one person, so we take our time. Approximate times:

    • Exterior: ~1.5 hours for sedans and SUVs, ~2.5 hours for trucks and 3-row SUVs.
    • Interior: ~3.5 hours for any vehicle.
    • Full Detail: ~5 hours for sedans and SUVs, ~6 hours for trucks and 3-row SUVs.
    • Heavy add-ons: heavy pet hair or heavy stain/odor removal adds about 30–45 minutes each.
    • Apex Membership visits: about 1 hour for a wash, about 1–1.5 hours for an interior refresh, and about 2 hours for a wash + interior refresh.

    These are estimates. A car in rough shape can take longer, and one that's kept up can take less. When you book, we'll give you an expected finish time. You don't need to stay outside with us.

    What if it rains or it's freezing?

    We'll reschedule exterior work for free. In winter (December 15 to March 1) we only do Interior details: it's too cold to wash outside, since water freezes on the paint and sealant won't cure. Exterior and Full Details are back March 1. Apex Membership follows the same winter dates: Wash plans pause with no charge, Wash + interior members get interior-only visits billed at the Interior plan price, and Interior plans carry on as normal. Members get an email a week before winter starts and ends.

    How does Apex Membership work?
    1. Get a qualifying detail. An Exterior or Full Detail unlocks the Wash plan, an Interior or Full Detail unlocks the Interior plan, and a Full Detail unlocks Wash + interior.
    2. Pick your plan within 14 days. Choose what each visit covers and how often: monthly, every 2 weeks (10% off) or weekly (20% off).
    3. Billing starts a month later. Your card on file is charged one month after that detail, then on the same date every month. Detail on the 18th, billed every 18th.
    4. Book your visits. Once each month is paid, pick your days and times from the open slots.

    Good to know

    • Membership is always paid by card in your Apex account. Paid your first detail in cash? You have 14 days to add a card.
    • Visits are maintenance cleans that keep it looking like it did after the detail. If it needs more (heavy spills, mud, pet hair), we'll quote it before we start.
    • Missed visits: if we cancel or the weather does, you get that visit's full value back as account credit (or a refund if you prefer). Skip with at least 48 hours' notice for 50% credit; later than that or a no-show gets no credit.
    • Interior-only plans just need an outlet; washes need a spigot too.
    • 3-month minimum (finish your 3 months, or cancel early for a fee of half the remaining payments), then pause or cancel anytime. Prices may change with 30 days' notice. Membership Terms
    How do I pay?

    After the job, once you've walked around the car and you're happy with it. Pay by card or cash in person, or save a card on file when you book and we'll charge it automatically after the detail. Apex Membership is always billed to a card on file.

    Our prices are before New Jersey sales tax (6.625%). The tax is added to your bill and shown on its own line on your estimate and receipt. Tips aren't taxed.

    What if I'm not happy with it?

    We walk the car with you before you pay. If something isn't right, tell us then, or within 48 hours with photos by text or email, and we'll come back and fix the areas the package covers at no charge.

    Can I reschedule or cancel?

    Yes, free up to 24 hours before. Use the link in your confirmation email. Closer than that, text or call us at (732) 206-7084.

    Do I need to be home?

    Only at the start, to hand us the keys and show us the spigot and outlet, and at the end for the walk-around and payment. You don't need to stay outside with us. If you can't be there, text us and we'll work it out.

    How should I get my car ready?

    Take out valuables and anything you don't want moved, like car seats or gear in the trunk. Park where we can reach it with a hose and an extension cord. That's it. We bring everything else.
